## Simple capture routine

Use the phone’s main camera at 1×. Clean the lens and use natural light where possible. Take one vertical and one horizontal frame of the same scene. Keep the action comfortably inside the frame so the website can crop around it. Avoid digital zoom and portrait blur around hands or equipment. Choose the best 2–3 frames, not dozens of near-duplicates.

Only photograph an activity already appropriate for the patient. Obtain permission to photograph and separate written permission to publish identifiable patient imagery for marketing. Record the release in the authorized practice system, not in the public repository. If permission is absent, use yourself or a consenting demonstration model. Avoid visible records, names, screens, mail, and other identifying household details.

## Label files for easy placement

Use descriptive names such as neck-movement-home-visit-01.jpg. Keep patient names and dates of care out of filenames. Retain the original image. When uploaded for the site, verify subject, alt text, image size, and the relevant page before publication.
